ABOUT THE HOME

Faith, care and hope.

Bobasi Faith Hope Home is based in Kisii County, Kenya. We seek to provide children with care, support and opportunities that help them grow in a stable and encouraging environment.

Our vision is to see children develop confidence, knowledge and practical abilities so that their circumstances do not define the limits of their future.
